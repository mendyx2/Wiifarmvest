Executive Action Summary

Wiifarmvest Technical Audit & Compensation Alignment
Target: Wiifarm Cooperative Society of Nigeria
Reference: Blueprint v1.0 & Audit Findings
Date: October 2026 • Single-Page Brief
Executive Summary: The core platform handles member registrations, e-wallets, and banking reliably. However, immediate intervention is required to halt critical bonus overpayments (money leakage), fix an affiliate underpayment bug, and implement missing leadership and reward features required by the business blueprint.
1. Critical Financial Risks: Stop Money Leakage & Payout Errors (Immediate)
  • Indirect Referral Overpayment Severe Cash Leakage (+8%)
    Current State: The system pays 13% across 5 upline levels (6%, 3%, 2%, 1%, 1%), whereas the business blueprint strictly caps this pool at 5.0%. Total acquisition payouts currently exceed 30% against a 22% limit.
    Required Fix: Recalibrate the 5 upline tiers to total exactly 5.0% (e.g., 2%, 1%, 1%, 0.5%, 0.5%) to immediately stop profit erosion.
  • Registration Key-In Bonus 100% Budget Overrun
    Current State: The data entry bonus is hardcoded at 2.0% per enrollment instead of the blueprint-mandated 1.0%, doubling planned costs.
    Required Fix: Update the payout rate from 2.0% down to 1.0% across all member registrations.
  • Affiliate Store Commission Bug Severe Underpayment Glitch
    Current State: When a marketer sells a retail product with a percentage commission (e.g., 10% on a ₦40,000 purchase = ₦4,000), the system mistakenly credits a flat ₦10.
    Required Fix: Correct the store commission payout so marketers automatically receive their legitimate percentage earnings.
2. Stability & Crash Prevention: Repair Broken Features (Within 1 Week)
  • Leadership Bonus System Crashes System Outage Risk
    Current State: The leadership bonus module references core database components that were never created. Triggering this calculation crashes the platform.
    Required Fix: Build the missing database structures so the 4% leadership bonus can run smoothly without error.
  • Repeat Purchase Bonuses Disabled Feature Turned Off
    Current State: The feature that awards upline sponsors when existing members purchase additional products was manually disabled in the system.
    Required Fix: Re-enable the upline repeat purchase pipeline so ongoing sales volume is properly credited.
3. Missing Blueprint Features: Deliver Promised Member Benefits (2–3 Weeks)
  • Stockist Sponsor Bonus (3.0%) Missing Incentive
    Current State: The blueprint promises a 3% reward to members who recruit a stockist store, but the software has no mechanism to track or pay this.
    Required Fix: Link stockists to their sponsoring members in the database and automate the 3% monthly override.
  • Global Profit-Sharing Pool (1.25%) Missing Distribution
    Current State: A 1.25% revenue share for top achievers is stipulated in the blueprint but currently has no tracking or payout tool.
    Required Fix: Implement a quarterly profit-sharing pool calculator for qualified leaders.
  • Wholesale Carton Packaging Option Wholesale Requirement
    Current State: Inventory only tracks individual pieces rather than standard 15-pack cartons specified in operations.
    Required Fix: Add wholesale carton units (1 carton = 15 packs) to product catalog and stockist ordering forms.
4. Financial Governance & Pool Separation (Phase 4)
  • Automated Revenue Split Ledgers: Currently, all incoming revenue enters a single account and bonuses are paid out of general cashflow. Program the system to automatically divide all revenue into the 4 designated pools (30% Company, 10% Executive, 5% Operations, 55% Compensation Reserve) before payouts occur to ensure absolute financial safety.
